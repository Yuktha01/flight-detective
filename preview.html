Why These 3 Picks Are Clean & Practical

1. Mobile Space Economy

Instead of 4 vertical cards consuming over 400px of height, these options consume either 0px (Copilot) or ~34px (Runway Pill Rail). Flight briefing results remain immediately visible.

2. Subtle Motion Physics

No tumbling letter scrambles or artificial countdown timers. Motion is restricted to smooth opacity cross-fades (0.35s), native touch inertia, and a 0.26s cubic-bezier tray transition.

3. Editorial Noir Palette

Uses calm studio white titanium hairlines (rgba(255,255,255,0.08)), flat noir cards (#101522), and cool ice blue (#93c5fd). Zero harsh green glows.
